
  Priya (Series B PM), Marcus (founder/CEO), Dana (Head of Product) — all three,        
  unprompted, landed on the same diagnosis. Your gut ("feels a bit weird") is correct
  and they named exactly where.                                                         
                                                                                        
  What they all said                                                                    
                                                                                        
  The vision is right. The surface is dressed as art when users need a pile.            
  - The ontology — promises, blockers, requests, shifts, signals — is the correct       
  primitive set. Nobody argued with it. This is the thing Linear/Notion/Slack get wrong.
  - The spatial "Team Field" is a demo asset. Priya: "spatial canvases die at ~50       
  objects, Miro is a graveyard." Marcus: "on an iPhone between calls it's hostile."     
  Dana: "gorgeous for one team, useless across four."                                   
  - Voice Orb as hero is wrong for ~80% of work contexts (open offices). Make it
  optional, text primary.                                                               
  - "Tensions" / "field" language launders concrete mechanics into vibes. Marcus: "reads
   like a VC tweet thread after ayahuasca." Rename or make the mechanics explicit.
                                                                                        
  The one killer feature all three named independently            
                                                                                        
  Drift / contradiction detection across artifacts and people.                          
  - Priya: "PRD says X, Figma says Y, Linear epic says Z, last Gong call said W — flag
  the divergence."                                                                      
  - Marcus: "You told Dev the refactor was descoped 9 days ago; 2 engineers have since
  written 1,400 lines assuming it's alive; Priya told the customer it ships Q2."        
  - Dana: "Team A's field says 'we own onboarding,' Team B is quietly building it. No   
  dashboard catches that."                                                           
                                                                                        
  This is the thing only an AI-native tool can do, and it's the one that turns
  evaluators into evangelists. If Lattice ships only one thing well, ship this.         
                                                                  
  What's structurally missing (concrete, ranked)                                        
                                                                  
  1. Passive capture — Slack DMs/channels, Granola/calls, Gmail, calendar → auto-draft  
  promises/blockers/decisions, one-tap confirm. Without this, Lattice is another thing 
  to feed, and it dies.                                                                 
  2. Linear two-way sync. Nobody is migrating 800 issues. Lattice promise → Linear issue
   when concrete; Linear status → updates Lattice object.                               
  3. Morning brief / "what changed since Friday" — 6 bullets, list form, before Slack.
  Replaces the weekly reconstruction ritual.                                            
  4. List view as good as Linear's. Canvas is a secondary lens, not the home.
  5. Cross-team / portfolio layer (the meta-field). Promises between teams, cross-team  
  dependency decay, contradiction between teams' stated ownership. Without this, Lattice
   is single-team and Dana never opens it.                                              
  6. Assumption Ledger. What was claimed true in Jan; flag when field activity          
  contradicts it.                                                                       
  7. Private/shared boundary that's visually obvious and trusted. Senior ICs and
  managers hedge for political reasons — if the tool makes hedging legible without their
   consent, adoption collapses above IC4. Dana was emphatic on this.
  8. Mobile. Actually mobile, not responsive web.                                       
  9. Promise extraction ≥90% accuracy or users clean up phantom commitments and quit.   
                                                                                        
  The honest reframe                                                                    
                                                                                        
  Your vision isn't broken — it's buried under aesthetic choices (spatial canvas, voice 
  orb, "field/tensions" language) that signal "art project" to the exact PMs you want as
   users. The underlying thesis — org state as a queryable live object, with an AI that 
  reads intent and surfaces contradictions across artifacts — is the unfair advantage.
  The current UI hides it.

  Suggested next move: cut the hero-surface spatial canvas down to a secondary view.    
  Make the primary surface a list + morning brief. Build one passive integration (Slack)
   and ship drift detection against it as the headline demo. That's a product PMs beg   
  their CEO for. Everything else is a wrapper.
